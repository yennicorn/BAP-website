About the Program

Shaping Future Mental Health Professionals

The Bachelor of Arts in Psychology at Ilocos Sur Polytechnic State College - Tagudin Campus represents our commitment to excellence in psychological education and mental health advocacy.

Our program provides a comprehensive understanding of human behavior, mental processes, and psychological principles through an integrated curriculum that balances theoretical knowledge with hands-on practical experience.

Students engage in rigorous coursework, research projects, field work, and community immersion programs designed to develop critical thinking, analytical skills, and ethical foundations essential for addressing contemporary mental health challenges.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our program, admissions, and student life.

Applicants must be high school graduates or have completed Senior High School. Required documents include Form 138 (Report Card), Birth Certificate, Certificate of Good Moral Character, 2x2 ID photos, and entrance examination results. Transfer students need transcript of records and honorable dismissal.

The Bachelor of Arts in Psychology is a four-year degree program consisting of eight semesters. Students must complete all required courses, practicum, and thesis requirements to graduate.

Graduates can pursue careers as guidance counselors, HR specialists, research assistants, community workers, case managers, and mental health advocates. With further studies, graduates can become licensed psychologists, psychometricians, or pursue graduate studies in specialized areas of psychology.

Yes, our Bachelor of Arts in Psychology program is accredited by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and follows the prescribed curriculum standards for psychology programs in the Philippines.

As a state college, ISPSC offers affordable quality education. Tuition fees vary depending on the number of units and are subject to government policies. Scholarships and financial assistance programs are available for qualified students. Please contact the admissions office for current rates.

We offer blended learning options combining face-to-face and online classes. The program includes hands-on laboratory work, practicum, and community immersion that require in-person participation. Contact the department for specific course delivery modes.
